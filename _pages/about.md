Hi! I am Yu Junzhe, an undergratuate student at School of Information and Science (SIST) in ShanghaiTech University, under the supervision of [Prof. Yuqi Chen](https://as3r-lab.com/).

My current work centers on the safe and ethical use of Large Language Models (LLMs), with a particular focus on detecting and mitigating malicious prompt attacks, such as jailbreaking, prompt injection, and sponge samples. Additionally, I am interested in the interpretability and robustness of LLMs, which could lead to improved practices for their responsible deployment.

**I am currently looking for a PhD position!** If you are interested in my research, please don't hesitate to contact me at [yujzh1@shanghaitech.edu.cn](mailto:yujzh1@shanghaitech.edu.cn).
